The research question

Can a model learn the defect,
not just the project?

Security archives contain many vulnerable functions, but few matched examples of what changed when the defect was fixed.

  1. !

    The source shortcut

    When a data source contributes only vulnerable examples, its formatting and naming conventions can become a shortcut for the label.

  2. !

    The patch sensitivity gap

    A model can rank functions well overall while assigning a higher risk score to a repaired function than to its vulnerable original.
